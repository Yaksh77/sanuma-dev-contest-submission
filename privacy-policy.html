Summary: Robuzta Tech Labs collects only the minimum data necessary to provide its services. We do not sell your data. We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

1. Overview

Robuzta Tech Labs ("Robuzta", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of visitors to our website (robuzta.com), repair customers, and business clients. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have over your data.

This policy is governed by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India and is also designed to align with internationally recognised principles including those of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Contact form submissions: name, company, email, phone, device issue description, and service interest.
  • Repair booking requests: device model, fault description, and preferred pickup date/time.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: email address.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Server access logs: IP address (anonymised after 24 hours), browser type, referring URL, and pages visited.
  • Theme preference: stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Never transmitted to our servers.

We do not use third-party analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Hotjar, etc.) on this site. We do not collect behavioural tracking data.

3. How We Use Data

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • Repair enquiries: To respond to repair booking requests within 1 business hour.
  • Service delivery: To coordinate device pickup, diagnostics, and repair notifications.
  • Newsletter: To send device care articles and service announcements (opt-in only; unsubscribe available in every email).
  • Site security: Server logs to detect and prevent malicious activity.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third party. We may share data with:

  • Email providers: For delivering responses to your enquiries (data processed under appropriate data processing agreements).
  • Legal authorities: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights or safety.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses only a single localStorage entry — robuzta-theme — to remember your light/dark mode preference. This is a functional storage entry, not a tracking cookie, and contains no personally identifiable information. No third-party cookies are set by this website.

6. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.
  • Right to grievance redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@robuzta.com.

7. Data Retention

Contact enquiry data is retained for a maximum of 24 months, after which it is permanently deleted unless a contractual relationship has been established. Recruitment data for unsuccessful candidates is deleted after 12 months. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All data transmitted to our systems is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 or higher.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and professional use. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, contact us immediately at support@robuzta.com.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy periodically. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify newsletter subscribers by email.
