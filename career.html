Engineering Culture

What Working Here Looks Like

Deep Technical Ownership

You own your systems end-to-end — from PLC register design through cloud ingestion to the user-facing dashboard. No hand-offs mid-way through.

Tight, Senior-Heavy Teams

We deliberately stay small. Each engineer works directly with domain experts, not within layers of process. Code reviews are substantive, not ceremonial.

Internship to Full-Time Pipeline

Our internship programme is structured, mentored, and directly feeds our hiring pipeline. Interns ship production code, not tutorial demos.
