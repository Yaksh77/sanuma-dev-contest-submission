Diagnostics Lab

Component-Level Logic Board Micro-soldering

We fix motherboard issues that other repair stores claim are unfixable. Our technicians inspect motherboard circuits under high-powered stereomicroscopes to isolate blown capacitors, corroded pads, and short-circuited chips.

Key Features:

  • Micro-soldering trace reconstruction and copper pad repairs.
  • Power Management IC (PMIC) and charging chip replacements.
  • Diagnostic resistance mapping of major power rails.
  • Microscopic chemical cleaning for water-damaged boards.
Measurable Outcome: Successful logic board restoration on over 92% of liquid-spill repair cases, avoiding expensive board replacements.

Micro-soldering Station
