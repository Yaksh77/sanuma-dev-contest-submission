Who We Are

Engineering Operational Trust

Founded in 2018, Sanuma India Pvt. Ltd. delivers robust controls and customized cloud platforms to industrial leaders.

Bridging Operational Telemetry and Cloud Code

Sanuma was established with a singular vision: to resolve the communication friction between industrial hardware (OT) and enterprise software systems (IT). We realized that manufacturing units were rich in sensors but starved of aggregated analytics.

Over the past 8 years, we have assembled a multi-disciplinary laboratory of control engineers, PLC programmers, database designers, and cloud developers. We do not build simple templates—we write the code that coordinates automated machinery with back-end enterprise architectures.

Our Core Principles

  • Operational Integrity

    Safety is non-negotiable. Every line of PLC code and panel layout complies with IEC 61508 safety standards.

  • Crawlable Predictability

    We construct software frameworks to be structurally clear, performant, and visible to inspection crawlers.

  • Long-Term Engineering SLA

    We provide structured post-commissioning service contracts to guarantee near-zero systems latency.
