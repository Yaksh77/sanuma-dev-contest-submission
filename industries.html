Sectors We Empower

Vertical Engineering Solutions

We resolve distinct mechanical bottlenecks, safety risks, and database constraints across four key industries.

Manufacturing

Automotive Components & Assembly

Automotive assembly lines represent high-speed coordination of multiple tool systems. A single PLC cycle delay halts output. We deploy optimized ladder logic and telemetry databases to align operations.

  • The Challenge: Siloed cycle diagnostics and tooling wear halts lines.
  • Our Solution: Extracting cycle telemetry directly from PLC registries to construct real-time Bottleneck Analytics consoles.
  • Technology: Siemens Simatic S7, OPC-UA servers, custom dashboard overlays.

Case Profile: Assembly Optimization

Deployed at Vikas Components, we routed 40 PLC processor nodes into an aggregated SQL database, allowing engineers to identify bottleneck patterns instantly and improve cycle times by 14%.

Case Profile: High-Throughput Portals

Distribution

Logistics & Warehouse Management

Modern distribution centers handle immense volumes of tracking transactions. A lagging inventory write system blocks warehouse sorting. We build secure cloud backends to optimize order routing.

  • The Challenge: Peak hour transactional spikes locking database tables, halting scanner gates.
  • Our Solution: Decentralizing API loads via memory caches (Redis) and event brokers (Kafka) to process orders asynchronously.
  • Technology: Redis clustering, Node-free static integrations, secure REST APIs.
Heavy Industry

Metals & Steel Processing

Processing hot steel slab grids involves high temperatures and chemical hazards. Human calibration is high-risk. We design automated, fail-safe PLC loops and predictive thermal telemetry.

  • The Challenge: Preventing bearing failures in high-vibration draft fans.
  • Our Solution: Accelerometer sensors reading bearings vibration frequency curves to predict structural wear.
  • Technology: Bearing vibration telemetry, Machine Learning anomaly scoring.

Case Profile: Failure Avoidance

Deployed at Aura Steel, our ML bearing scanners predicted a bearing cage failure 48 hours before breakdown, triggering scheduled service and saving significant equipment downtime costs.

Energy & Power

Utilities & Grid Telemetry

Electricity grid distribution relies on load balance monitoring. A lag in power metrics leads to overloading. We build telemetry collection layers to display line metrics.

  • The Challenge: Gathering sub-second load telemetries from remote substations.
  • Our Solution: Establishing secure MQTT grid lines and visual load balancer widgets.
  • Technology: MQTT over TLS, dashboard interfaces.
